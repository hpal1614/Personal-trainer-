# 07 — Tracking: Measure, Adjust, Win

What gets measured gets managed. A professional coach doesn't guess — they collect data weekly and adjust based on trends. This is your control system. Without it, you're flying blind.

---

## Baseline (do this before Day 1)

Record these once, today:

- [ ] **Bodyweight** (morning, after bathroom, before eating/drinking)
- [ ] **Waist circumference** at the navel (the single most useful tape measurement for fat loss)
- [ ] **Photos** — front, side, back; same lighting, same spot, relaxed. *This is the most honest progress tracker there is.*
- [ ] **Key lift numbers** — your current working weights/reps on squat, bench, deadlift/RDL, row, overhead/shoulder press
- [ ] *(Optional)* additional measurements: chest, arms, thighs, hips
- [ ] *(Optional)* body-fat estimate (calipers, DEXA, or honest photo comparison)

---

## Daily tracking (30 seconds)

| Metric | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Morning bodyweight** | Same conditions every day. Log it. Expect daily noise — that's normal. |
| **Food** | Log everything (MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, etc.). Hit protein and calorie targets. Accuracy here = results. |
| **Steps** | From phone or watch. Hit your daily target. |
| **Training** | Log every working set: weight × reps × RIR. Beat last session's numbers. |

---

## Weekly check-in (your most important habit)

Same day each week (e.g., Sunday morning). Spend 10 minutes:

1. **Calculate your 7-day average bodyweight** — *this* is your real weight, not any single day.
2. **Compare to last week's average.** Are you losing 0.5–1.0% of bodyweight?
3. **Re-measure your waist.**
4. **Take progress photos** (every 1–2 weeks is plenty).
5. **Review your training log** — are lifts holding or climbing?
6. **Rate your biofeedback** (below).
7. **Make ONE adjustment if needed** (decision rules below).

---

## Biofeedback checklist (rate 1–5 each week)

These tell you whether the deficit is sustainable or you're digging too deep:

- 😴 **Sleep quality** — falling/staying asleep, waking rested?
- ⚡ **Energy / mood** — stable, or irritable and flat?
- 💪 **Gym performance** — lifts holding, or dropping multiple sessions?
- 🍽️ **Hunger** — manageable, or constant and overwhelming?
- ❤️ **Resting heart rate** — stable, or creeping up (a fatigue signal)?
- 🤒 **Recovery** — recovering between sessions, or always sore / getting sick?

**Multiple low scores stacking up = you're under-recovering.** Trigger a **diet break** and/or **deload**.

---

## The decision rules (what a coach does with the data)

| What the data shows | The adjustment |
|---------------------|----------------|
| Losing 0.5–1.0% BW/week, lifts holding, biofeedback good | ✅ **Do nothing.** It's working. Don't fix what isn't broken. |
| Weight stalled 2 weeks, biofeedback fine | ⬇️ Cut ~150–200 cal (from carbs/fat) **or** add a cardio session / 2,000 steps. |
| Losing too fast (>1.5% BW), lifts dropping | ⬆️ Add ~150 cal back (protein/carbs). Protect the muscle. |
| Biofeedback tanking, multiple low scores | 🛑 **Diet break:** 1 week at maintenance + deload. Then resume. |
| Waist shrinking but scale flat | ✅ **Great news** — you're recomping (losing fat, holding/adding muscle). Keep going. |
| Scale dropping but waist not, lifts falling | ⚠️ Likely losing muscle. Raise protein, slow the deficit, check sleep. |

> **Golden rule: change ONE variable at a time, then wait ~2 weeks to read the result.** Change three things at once and you'll never know which one worked.

---

## When to end Phase 1 (the cut)

Transition out of the cut when **any** of these is true:
- You've **hit your target leanness** (the look you wanted in the mirror/photos).
- You've been cutting **12–16 weeks** and need a real reset.
- **Biofeedback is chronically poor** despite diet breaks — your body needs a maintenance phase.

Then move to the **Maintenance Bridge** (reverse diet back to maintenance over 2–4 weeks) before any **Lean Gain** phase. *Don't* jump straight from a deep deficit into a big surplus — you'll regain fat fast.
